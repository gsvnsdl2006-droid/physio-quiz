How to play

  1. Join the room. Enter the host’s six-digit PIN and pick a nickname. Tap Ready when you’re set.
  2. Lock in an answer. Everyone gets one choice per question. The answer and explanation appear when the clock runs out or everyone connected has answered.
  3. Build your score. A correct answer earns 1,000 points. From your third consecutive correct answer, each correct answer adds 200 bonus points.
