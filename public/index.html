THE PLAYBOOK

How to play

  1. Join the room. Enter the host’s six-digit PIN and pick a nickname. Tap Ready when you’re set.
  2. Race for points. Correct answers score in the order the server receives them: 1,000, 900, 800, down to 100 from tenth place onward. Wrong answers never take a rank. Three or more correct in a row adds 200.
  3. Check the round card. Special rules appear before you answer. Everyone can score, and most rounds lock in one choice.
